BRIGHTVOICE AAC - WINDOWS / LAPTOP APP
======================================

BrightVoice is an offline augmentative and alternative communication (AAC)
starter app for Windows laptops and touch devices. It is designed to support
communication and vocabulary practice for autistic children and other children
who benefit from visual communication.

HOW TO START ON WINDOWS
-----------------------
Double-click:
  BrightVoice-AAC-Windows.exe

The Windows executable opens the app in your default browser. The app works
offline and no login is required. The .cmd launcher is also included as a
fallback.

FEATURES
--------
- Large picture-based communication cards
- Sentence builder with Speak, Undo, and Clear actions
- Fixed quick words: Yes, No, More, Help, Stop, Please
- Frozen core-word strip that remains available while browsing categories
- Starter, growing, and full vocabulary levels for gradual vocabulary expansion
- Vocabulary categories: needs, feelings, people, food, activities, places,
  things, describing words, question words, and core words
- Indian breakfast vocabulary page with foods such as idli, dosa, paratha,
  pongal, and sambar
- Favorites category for frequently used words
- Keyboard mode with quick phrase suggestions
- Learn Words mode for simple vocabulary matching practice
- Caregiver area to add and remove personal words, adjust card size, set a
  child's name, and adjust speech speed
- Settings and personal words saved locally on the laptop

IMPORTANT NOTE
--------------
BrightVoice is a starter communication tool, not medical advice or a substitute
for an individualized AAC assessment. A speech-language pathologist, educator,
or occupational therapist can help adapt the vocabulary and access method for a
specific child.
